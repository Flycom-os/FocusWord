# Blog Posts User Guide

## Introduction to Blog Posts

Blog Posts are used for creating engaging entries on your company blog. This module allows you to manage all aspects of your blog content, from drafting new posts to organizing existing ones, ensuring your audience stays informed and engaged.

## The Main Blog Page (`/admin/blog`)

Navigating to `/admin/blog` will present you with the main overview of all your existing blog posts. This page serves as your central hub for managing your blog content.

*   **List View:** Displays a comprehensive list of all blog posts, showing key information such as **Title**, **Author**, **Status**, **Categories**, and **Last Updated** date.
*   **Create Blog Post:** Click the **Create Blog Post** button (usually located at the top right) to start crafting a new blog entry.
*   **Edit:** To modify an existing blog post, locate it in the list and click the **Edit** icon (often represented by a pencil) next to its entry.
*   **Delete:** To remove a blog post, click the **Delete** icon (often a trash can) next to its entry. A confirmation prompt will appear to prevent accidental deletions.
*   **Filtering and Searching:** Utilize the search bar and filter options (e.g., by **Status**, **Category**, **Author**) to quickly find specific blog posts within your list.

## Creating and Editing Blog Posts

When you create a new blog post or edit an existing one, you'll be directed to a two-column editor designed for efficient content creation and management.

### Main Content Column

This is where you'll focus on the primary content of your blog post.

*   **Title:** Enter the main heading of your blog post. This is crucial for search engines and readability.
*   **Excerpt:** Provide a brief summary or teaser of your blog post. This often appears in blog listings or search results.
*   **Content Editor:** A powerful block-based editor allows you to structure your content flexibly.
    *   **Media Embedding:** Easily embed images, videos, and other media files directly into your blog post content.
    *   **Slider Embedding:** Integrate sliders to showcase image galleries or rotating content sections within your post.

### Settings Sidebar

The right-hand sidebar provides various settings to configure your blog post's behavior and appearance.

*   **Status:** Control the visibility of your blog post:
    *   **Draft:** The blog post is saved but not public.
    *   **Published:** The blog post is live and visible to your audience.
    *   **Archived:** The blog post is no longer public but kept for record-keeping.
*   **Slug:** This is the URL-friendly name for your blog post (e.g., `yourwebsite.com/blog/your-blog-post-slug`). It's often auto-generated from the title but can be customized.
*   **Categories:** Assign one or more categories to your blog post for better organization and navigation.
*   **Template:** Choose a specific layout template for your blog post if multiple options are available.
*   **Featured Image/Slider:** Select a prominent image or a slider to represent your blog post. This is often displayed in blog listings or at the top of the post.
*   **SEO (Search Engine Optimization):** Configure settings to improve your blog post's visibility in search engine results. This typically includes:
    *   **Meta Title:** The title that appears in search engine results.
    *   **Meta Description:** A brief summary that appears under the meta title in search results.
    *   **Keywords:** Relevant keywords to help search engines understand your content.
*   **Enable Feedback:** Toggle this option to allow or disallow comments/feedback on your blog post.
*   **Payment Method:** Attach a specific payment method if your blog post is part of a monetized content strategy (e.g., premium content).
